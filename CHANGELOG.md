> raises TypeError at registry setup, blocking database load:
>   TypeError: Many2many fields purchase.order.x_studio_project_photos
>   and purchase.order.x_studio_photos use the same table and columns
> Fix: give each such m2m field an explicit (relation_table,
> host_column, target_column) so join-table names are unique.
> Scaffolder patched to emit the explicit relation on every m2m
> declaration; future scaffolds will not regress.
