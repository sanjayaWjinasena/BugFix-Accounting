**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:x_rm_sales_prod_purch -->
A line of the 'Sales - Production - Purchase Report' run from a Sales Report Model, giving per product the sales, production and purchase quantities with base price and unit cost. Lines are rebuilt by the 'SRM - RPT - Sales - Production - Purchase Report' action, whose code calls datetime.strptime on a date and likely fails at runtime.
