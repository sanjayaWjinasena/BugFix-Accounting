### `account.bank.statement.line` — Bank Statement Line

*Extends a model created by `account`.* Python: `models/account_bank_statement_line.py`.

**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:account.bank.statement.line -->
This repo adds 105 non-stored placeholder fields to bank statement lines. Each one copies the name of a Studio field on Journal Entry. They have no related path or compute, so they are always empty on statement lines. The repo also adds three record rules: a company rule and open-access rules for Accounting Billing and Point of Sale users.
