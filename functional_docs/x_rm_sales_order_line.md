**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:x_rm_sales_order_line -->
A line of the 'Sales Details for Incentive Calc.' report run from a Sales Report Model, giving customer, customer group, sales order, invoice, product, category, quantity, prices, discount and net value. Lines are recreated by the 'SRM - RPT - Sales Details for Incentive Calc.' action from income journal items on the As On Date.
