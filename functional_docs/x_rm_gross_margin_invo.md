**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:x_rm_gross_margin_invo -->
An invoice details line of the 'Project Gross Margin' report run from a Sales Report Model, giving the customer invoice, its date, posting status, payment status, invoice status text and amount. Lines are rebuilt by the 'SRM - RPT - Project Gross Margin' action.
