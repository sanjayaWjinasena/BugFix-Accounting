### `rm_production_varian` — RM Production Variance

*Created by this repo.* Python: `models/rm_production_varian.py`, `models/x_rm_production_varian_gap.py`. Record name field: `name`.

Other repos that use this model: `server action BugFix-Studio-Misc.server_action_2799_mst_odoo_data_clean_up` (BugFix-Studio-Misc)

**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:rm_production_varian -->
A line of the 'Production Job Variance' report run from a Sales Report Model, giving per manufacturing order and component the BoM, estimated, consumed and produced quantities, the variance, average cost and total cost. Lines are recreated by the 'SRM - RPT - Production Job Variance' action for raw-material moves with a non-zero variance.
<!-- /SUMMARY -->
