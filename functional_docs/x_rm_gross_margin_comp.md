### `x_rm_gross_margin_comp` — RM Gross Margin - Comparison

*Created by this repo.* Python: `models/x_rm_gross_margin_comp.py`, `models/x_rm_gross_margin_comp_gap.py`. Record name field: `x_name`.

**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:x_rm_gross_margin_comp -->
A comparison line of the 'Project Gross Margin' report run from a Sales Report Model, giving per product and sales order the estimated, delivered, invoiced and delivered-not-invoiced amounts. Lines are rebuilt by the 'SRM - RPT - Project Gross Margin' action and shown in an inline list with summed columns.
