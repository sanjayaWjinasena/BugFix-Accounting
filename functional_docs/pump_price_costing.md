### `pump_price_costing` — Pump Price Costing

*Created by this repo.* Python: `models/pump_price_costing.py`, `models/x_pump_price_costing_gap.py`. Record name field: `name`.

Other repos that use this model: `server action BugFix-Studio-Misc.server_action_2799_mst_odoo_data_clean_up` (BugFix-Studio-Misc)

**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:pump_price_costing -->
One line of the 'Costing Work Sheet' report run from a Sales Report Model, giving the cost build-up and proposed selling price of a pump product: material, labour and overhead costs, idling rates, contingency, profit mark-up, SSCL, discounts and net selling price. Lines are deleted and recreated by the 'SRM - RPT - Costing Work Sheet' action for every pump product of the active company. They are viewed in a read-only list and through Costing Worksheet actions with pivot and graph views.
<!-- /SUMMARY -->
